CubeSat Detumbling with Magnetorquers
Numerical method and convergence
AE 370 Fall 2026 Project 1
Project idea
AE 370 Aerospace Numerical Methods Fall 2026 Project 1 studies a numerical method for a dy-
namical system. Our question is how initial angular rate, controller gain, and magnetic dipole
limit affect CubeSat detumbling time in an orbital magnetic field. A magnetorquer commands a
dipole moment. Its interaction with Earth’s magnetic field produces torque. The available torque
is perpendicular to the field, so changing field direction and initial geometry matter.
We will solve quaternion attitude and rigid body angular velocity equations using our own classi-
cal RK4 implementation. Nonlinear rotational coupling, orbital forcing, and actuator saturation
require numerical solution for the full problem. An exact special case will verify the implementation.
We will report detumbling time, success before a deadline, and saturation fraction. A missed
deadline is a failed case for this study. It does not prove that the spacecraft can never recover.
