\section{Conclusions}
The physics, inputs, numerical methods, and verification studies are separated in the implementation. Independent scalar and spherical rotation tests support first order Euler, second order Heun, and fourth order RK4 on smooth problems. Torque free energy and inertial momentum errors decrease with refinement. These results establish method verification for the stated cases. Engineering conclusions about controller gain, actuator capability, and detumbling time require the production field and controller, saturation studies, and a justified production time step.
