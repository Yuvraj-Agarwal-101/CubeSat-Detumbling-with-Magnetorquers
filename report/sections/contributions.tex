\section{Contributions}

The project was developed collaboratively, with each member owning a major technical part of the final integrated workflow.

\begin{itemize}

\item \textbf{Yuvraj Agarwal (Person 1, 25\%):} developed the engineering premise and shared project configuration, established the quaternion and rigid-body dynamics interfaces, organized the separation between physical modeling and numerical methods, and integrated the common numerical architecture and verification framework used by the team.

\item \textbf{Aleksandar Bargoz (Person 2, 25\%):} developed the scratch numerical integration workflow and numerical-method analysis, including convergence, stability, timestep-refinement, and output-resolution studies. His work also contributed the orbital magnetic-field and B-dot controller implementations that were incorporated into the final integrated model.

\item \textbf{Sidhant Jha (Person 3, 25\%):} performed the independent review and verification of the orbital magnetic-field and B-dot controller physics, including field-derivative, frame-convention, saturation, torque, and energy checks. He also integrated the teammate branches, verified the merged production model, and incorporated the accepted $0.25$ s production timestep and verification gate into the final workflow.

\item \textbf{Dibyansh Choudhury (Person 4, 25\%):} developed the engineering-study and post-processing framework, including deterministic case generation, detumbling-time and hold-interval detection, saturation and actuator metrics, numerical-diagnostic and refinement tools, parameter-sweep infrastructure, and reproducible figure generation. His workflow was used to execute and analyze the final targeted engineering study and to generate the engineering results included in the report.

\end{itemize}

All four members contributed to the integration, interpretation, and review of the final results. The agreed contribution allocation is \textbf{25\% per team member}.
