\section{Engineering results}
Engineering results remain pending. The dynamics, orbit and magnetic field,
controller, and numerical integrator must be connected and independently
verified before the engineering study can run. Synthetic pipeline fixtures
are excluded from the engineering conclusions.

The planned comparisons measure detumbling time conditional on success,
success fraction before the common deadline, and time weighted saturation
fraction. Each map will state the gain, initial spin, dipole limit, ensemble
size, and assumed physical parameters. Cases without a complete qualifying
hold interval will remain censored. Invalid simulation records will be
reported separately from valid cases that miss the deadline.

A paired comparison will use identical initial conditions for the orbital
and frozen inertial magnetic fields. The latter holds the inertial field
at its initial value and sets its inertial derivative to zero, while
continuing to transform it into the body frame.
