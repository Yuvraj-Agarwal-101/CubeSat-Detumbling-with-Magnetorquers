\subsection{Current evidence and outstanding results}
Engineering simulations have not been run. The full field and controller,
scratch RK4, independent verification, and accepted production step remain
required. The experiment pipeline is tested using explicitly labeled fixtures.
Fixture times and success rates are excluded from spacecraft conclusions.

The postprocessing verification samples $r(t)=4e^{-t}$ and
$d(t)=2e^{-t}$ on $0\le t\le4$, with rate threshold one, a hold of one,
and dipole limit one. These are dimensionless analytic test histories rather
than spacecraft data. Their exact recovery time is $\log4$, saturation
fraction is $(\log2)/4$, and commanded dipole effort is
$\log2+1-2e^{-4}$. Reducing output spacing from $0.25$ to $0.0078125$
reduces the measured recovery time error from $7.7096\times10^{-3}$ to
$7.5404\times10^{-6}$. The saturation fraction error changes from
$1.3402\times10^{-3}$ to $1.5276\times10^{-6}$. Errors need not decrease
by exactly four at each halving because the crossing moves within its
sampling cell. The data and optional verification figure are regenerated by
\texttt{studies.metric\_refinement}. These results check metric evaluation,
not the order of a time integrator.

The pending engineering figures are detumbling time and success maps at
stated gains, gain and saturation comparisons, and paired frozen versus
orbital field comparisons. Every figure must identify the geometry count,
physical assumptions, gain, actuator limits, and any censored or invalid
cases. Conditional time statistics must say that they include successful
cases only. All engineering conclusions remain pending these measured
comparisons.
