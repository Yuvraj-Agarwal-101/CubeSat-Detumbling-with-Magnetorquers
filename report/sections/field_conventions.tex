\subsection{Shared orbit and field conventions}
The canonical baseline is stored in \texttt{configs/baseline.json}. Its values and the callback contract define the implemented idealized magnetic field and controller connection. The real adapter, independent physics checks, merged-source numerical acceptance, and targeted engineering study are documented here. The field is an idealized project model rather than an epoch-specific measured prediction.

The orbit and dipole rotations use the inertial basis and active rotation matrices specified above. At \(t=0\), orbital and Earth rotation phases are their configured initial values. There is no calendar epoch in this idealized field. For a circular orbit, let \(u=nt+\phi\). Then
\[
\dot r_I=nr_oR_3(\Omega)R_1(i)
\begin{bmatrix}
-\sin u\\ \cos u\\ 0
\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
\dot{\hat r}=\dot r_I/r_o,
\qquad
\dot d_I=\omega_E e_3\times d_I.
\]
With \(g=d_I^T\hat r\), the derivative along the orbit obeys
\[
\dot g=\dot d_I^T\hat r+d_I^T\dot{\hat r},
\qquad
\dot B_I+b\dot d_I=3b(\dot g\hat r+g\dot{\hat r}).
\]
The scale \(b=B_{\rm eq}(R_E/r_o)^3\) is constant on this prescribed circular orbit. Both orbital motion and rotation of the dipole axis contribute to the field derivative.

The field callback returns the inertial field and this total derivative. The dynamics layer alone applies the body rotation correction. The controller receives \(B_B\) and \(\dot B_B\), uses the normalized gain definition, and applies vector norm saturation. Body rotation must not be counted twice. Dipole effort is \(\int\|m\|\,dt\), an actuator activity measure rather than electrical energy.

The Person 3 merged-source review verifies dipole scaling, equatorial and polar geometry, the analytic derivative against independently refined centered differences, and the callback contract. It also verifies saturation boundaries, magnetic torque geometry, the complete instantaneous dynamics, and fresh forcing and feedback at every real adapter RK4 stage. Orbital position and velocity repeat after an orbit; the rotating tilted magnetic field need not. The static inertial field energy identity does not imply monotonic rotational energy for every orbital field case. Historical timestep evidence must be regenerated on the merged numerical implementation before the engineering pilot.
